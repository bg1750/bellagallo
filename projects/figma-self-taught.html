Making it my own

The course was really just a starting skeleton: a homepage, about, catalog, and checkout. I took that foundation and kept building, adding more pages, more interactions and motion, a fuller color and type system, and my own copy throughout, until it felt like my design instead of an exercise. I picked it up in my free time whenever I could, learning Figma's components, auto layout, and prototyping as I went.
