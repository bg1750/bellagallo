SmartMotor Mixing Device

Programmed a Moog Animatics SmartMotor for Savannah River National Laboratory to drive a mixing device in torque mode, using torque ramp profiles for smooth, repeatable mixing cycles without mechanical shock.

Moog Animatics Torque Control Motion Control SmartMotor SMI Embedded Systems

A Moog Animatics SmartMotor, an integrated servo with an onboard controller, programmed to drive a mixing device with smooth, repeatable shaft rotation.

  • Torque-mode control, ramping output torque up and down instead of commanding position or velocity directly, for fine control of the load during acceleration and deceleration.
  • A set number of shaft rotations per mixing cycle, with ramp curves tuned for smooth start and stop and the cycle count tracked onboard.
  • Programmed and validated in the Moog Animatics SMI environment using the motor's native command language.
